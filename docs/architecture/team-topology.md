# Team Topology

The repository is organized as though a real product organization owns it.

## Product Application Team

Owns the web application, SaaS API, organizations, memberships, projects, profile management, and customer-facing activity.

## Identity and Security Team

Owns Kratos, Hydra, identity schemas, login/registration UX, recovery, MFA, lifecycle integration, and authorization policy.

## Data Plane Team

Owns Mammoth configuration, logical replication, routing, ordering, checkpoints, retries, dead letters, replay, and sink contracts.

## Messaging and Integrations Team

Owns transactional email, webhook delivery, audit storage, templates, provider adapters, and delivery observability.

## Platform and DevOps Team

Owns Compose, Kubernetes, Helm, OpenTofu, GitOps, CI/CD, secrets, observability, backups, SLOs, and operational runbooks.
