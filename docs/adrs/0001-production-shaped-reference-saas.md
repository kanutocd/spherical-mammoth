# ADR-0001: Build a Production-Shaped Reference SaaS

- Status: Accepted

## Decision

Build Spherical Mammoth as a real, usable SaaS-shaped application with a customer UI, identity lifecycle, independent services, and DevOps ownership boundaries.

The business domain remains intentionally small. Engineering behavior must remain credible enough to exercise Mammoth under realistic conditions.

## Consequences

- The project demonstrates Mammoth without looking like a synthetic benchmark.
- Product depth is constrained to avoid distracting from data-plane behavior.
- Operational scenarios and acceptance tests are first-class deliverables.
